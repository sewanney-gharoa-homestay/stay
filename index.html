Welcome to Your Mountain Home

Welcome to Sewanney Gharoa Homestay! Nestled amidst the peaceful hills and lush orange orchards of Upper Sittong. Our home offers a true sanctuary where mountain calm meets genuine Himalayan warmth. True to its name, you will experience a Gharoa (home-like) feeling that helps you escape the rush of everyday life, where guests are welcomed not merely as travelers, but embraced warmly as family.

Greet your mornings with golden sunrises over the snow-crowned peaks of Mt. Kanchenjunga (Himalayan mountain), crisp alpine air, and soothing birdsong echoing across the terraced green valleys. Savor delicious, wholesome regional meals prepared with organic locally produced and served fresh with heartfelt care. Whether you are relaxing in spotless, cozy rooms, exploring the tranquil surroundings, or watching the evening sky paint the hills in magical golden and twilight hues over hot cups of Darjeeling tea, our home promises an unforgettable escape into tranquility.

Places to Visit Near Stay

Scenic Vista
🌄

Ahaldara Viewpoint

Located a short distance away, this popular vantage point provides an incredible 360-degree panoramic view of the surrounding hills, tea gardens, and the majestic Kanchenjunga range.

Eco & Wildlife
🏞️

Namthing Pokhri (Lake)

A serene natural lake nestled in the Shelpu hills surrounded by pine forests, known locally as a peaceful habitat for the rare Himalayan Salamander.

River & Heritage
🌉

Jogighat Bridge & Riyang River

A picturesque steel suspension bridge built over the flowing Riyang Khola mountain stream, decorated with colorful fluttering prayer flags.

Village Orchards
🍊

Local Orange Orchards

Walk through the vibrant orange orchards of Ghalaytor Bazar and Shelpu, which paint the village in bright hues and sweet aromas during the winter harvest season.

Culture & Peace
🏯

Sittong Monastery (Upper Ghalaytar)

An ancient, culturally rich Buddhist monastery offering a peaceful spiritual ambiance alongside stunning valley views.

Nature & Adventure
💧

Jogikhola Waterfall & Rock Climbing

A scenic roadside waterfall and natural rock bed formation located along the local hills, perfect for day excursions and adventure photography.
